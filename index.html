About

I'm a first-year PhD student in Interdisciplinary Engineering at Northeastern University (Portland, ME), where my research sits at the intersection of aerospace materials, advanced manufacturing, and data-driven modeling. My work focuses on understanding the physics of polymer systems — how they diffuse, heal, and respond to laser energy — to enable next-generation composite manufacturing processes.

Prior to my PhD, I completed a BS in Aerospace Engineering at Virginia Tech (May 2025), where I spent three internship terms at NASA Langley Research Center's Advanced Materials and Processing Branch. That experience shaped my conviction that deep material understanding and computational modeling go hand in hand.

Outside the lab, I've led a 30-person team in national 3D-printed aircraft competitions, placing 2nd twice and winning Most Innovative Design in 2025. I'm also a strong believer that good science communicates — I've given several oral presentations at NASA symposia and am working toward my first first-author journal paper.

Polymer Diffusion & Healing
