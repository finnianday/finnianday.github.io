OATMEAL Project · NASA Langley / Northeastern · Jan 2026–present

Polymer diffusion & laser-induced surface engineering

The OATMEAL (Optimizing Automated Thermoplastic Manufacturing via Engineering Advanced Laminates) project develops cooling-rate-independent thermoplastic prepregs for laser-assisted Automated Fiber Placement (AFP). My role centers on the fundamental physics of how polymers interdiffuse at interfaces — the process that determines bond quality when a thermoplastic tape is consolidated onto a substrate.

I have coupled modeling and experimentation to optimize laser-ablation process parameters and capture ablation physics, and developed a predictive model linking polymer diffusion kinetics with laser–material interactions. This work is directed toward a first-author journal manuscript in Composites Part A.

Key question
How does laser fluence and pulse duration control the depth and selectivity of polymer ablation, and can we use diffusion models to predict healing at the newly exposed interface?
Methods
Polymer interdiffusion experiments · laser ablation parameter studies · rheometry · DSC · predictive modeling in Python/MATLAB

TTT Project · NASA Langley · May–Aug 2024

Machine learning for in-situ bondline NDE

Developed non-destructive methods to measure bonded carbon fiber joint thickness during autoclave cure. Built MATLAB GUIs for live analysis of ultrasonic amplitude and time-of-flight data, then trained SVMs and Gaussian process regression models that improved bondline measurement accuracy by 15%. Co-authored two journal papers currently under review.

SVM · GPR · Ultrasonic NDE · MATLAB · Autoclave cure
SUMAC Project · NASA Langley · Jan–May 2024

Sustainable flax fiber thermoplastic composites

Investigated flax fiber reinforced thermoplastics for advanced air mobility and commercial aircraft. Fabricated ASTM specimens using CNC machining, synthesized PVA hydrogels via freeze-thaw cycling, and characterized thermoplastic resins by TGA, DSC, and DMA across a range of temperatures and cooling rates.

Flax fiber · PVA hydrogels · TGA · DSC · DMA · Freeze-thaw synthesis
