Current PhD Research

OATMEAL Project · NASA Langley / Northeastern · 2025–Present

Polymer Diffusion & Laser-Induced Surface Engineering

The OATMEAL (Optimizing Automated Thermoplastic Manufacturing via Engineering Advanced Laminates) project develops cooling-rate-independent thermoplastic prepregs. My role centers on the physics governing how polymers interdiffuse at interfaces — a critical process that determines bond strength in laser-assisted Automated Fiber Placement (AFP).

I have optimized laser-ablation process parameters by coupling modeling and experimentation to capture ablation physics, and developed a predictive model linking polymer diffusion with laser–material interactions for controlled ablation. This work culminates in a first-author manuscript focused on model calibration of miscible polymers and their selective removal.

Polymer Interdiffusion Laser Ablation Thermoplastic AFP Predictive Modeling OATMEAL
Key Questions
How fast do polymers heal?
Quantifying diffusion timescales and healing behavior across varied polymer systems as a function of temperature and molecular weight.
What governs ablation selectivity?
Building coupled models that predict how laser fluence, wavelength, and material properties determine the ablation threshold.
Can we eliminate surface prep steps?
Replacing mechanical/chemical surface treatment with laser-engineered interfaces — reducing cost and enabling automated production.

NASA Langley
TTT Project

Machine Learning for Bondline Measurement

Developed non-destructive methods to measure bonded carbon fiber composite joint thickness during autoclave cure. Created MATLAB GUIs for live analysis of in-situ ultrasonic amplitude and time-of-flight data, then trained and implemented SVMs and Gaussian Process Regression models that improved bondline measurement accuracy by 15%.

Jan 2024 – May 2024
NASA Langley
SUMAC Project

Sustainable Flax Fiber Thermoplastic Composites

Investigated flax fiber reinforced thermoplastic composites for advanced air mobility applications. Fabricated and tested ASTM specimens using CNC machining, synthesized PVA hydrogels via freeze-thaw cycling, and characterized resin properties using TGA, DSC, and DMA. Work aimed at reducing the carbon footprint of structural aerospace materials.
