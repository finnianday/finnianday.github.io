Polishing samples at NASA Langley, 2024

I am broadly interested in understanding how aerospace materials behave at the critical interfaces that determine their performance. Whether its particles impacting a substrate at supersonic speeds, polymers diffusing into one another, or ultrasonic waves probing a material for defects, my work centers on how to predict and control what happens at material interfaces.

My PhD research investigates solid-state additive manufacturing through cold spray. My work centers on how processing parameters govern bonding mechanisms and interfacial quality across different material systems. Across all of them, the same underlying mechanisms play out at interfaces that are difficult to observe and hard to predict. Long term, I'm interested in applying data visualization and machine learning to materials characterization by using in-situ sensor data to predict process-structure-property relationships without destructive testing.

OATMEAL Project · NASA Langley / Northeastern · 2025–Present
Polymer Diffusion & Laser-Induced Surface Engineering

The OATMEAL (Optimizing Automated Thermoplastic Manufacturing via Engineering Advanced Laminates) project develops cooling-rate-independent thermoplastic prepregs for laser-assisted Automated Fiber Placement (AFP). My research centers on the polymer interdiffusion problem: how do miscible polymer systems diffuse across interfaces, and how does laser ablation modify surface chemistry to control that process?

I am developing predictive models — grounded in Fickian diffusion and reptation theory — that link processing parameters (laser power, scan speed, temperature history) to bond strength outcomes. A first-author manuscript is in preparation for Composites Part A: Applied Science and Manufacturing.

TTT Project · NASA Langley · 2024
Machine Learning for Composite Bondline NDE

Developed support vector machine and Gaussian process regression models to improve in-situ ultrasonic bondline thickness measurements during autoclave cure. Achieved a 15% improvement in time-of-flight prediction accuracy over baseline methods by incorporating cure cycle state variables as model inputs.

This work resulted in two co-authored papers currently under review at Research in Nondestructive Evaluation and the Journal of Nondestructive Evaluation.

SUMAC Project · NASA Langley · 2024
Sustainable Flax Fiber Thermoplastic Composites

Investigated flax fiber reinforced thermoplastics as sustainable alternatives for advanced air mobility structures. Synthesized polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) hydrogels via freeze-thaw cycling as a matrix system and characterized resin behavior using TGA, DSC, and DMA across a range of processing conditions.

Hume Center · NSEPC CubeSat · 2022–2024
CubeSat Power Systems — HokieSat IV

Led design and integration of the power subsystem for a 3U CubeSat mission. Built power budgets and conducted trade studies integrating the Electron Multiplying CCD (EMCCD) payload with mission-level power constraints. Presented findings at the Hume Center National Security Education Program Colloquium.
